Why teams choose us

The questions you'll ask before you trust anyone with this. And our answers.

“What if we pay and nothing changes?”

Our fees follow the result: subscriptions, success fees and gated builds. Never billed by the hour.

“We've been stuck in pilot before.”

We have agents in production across five industries and four countries. A first agent is usually live in five to eight weeks.

“Our people won't learn another tool.”

They don't have to. Agents work inside Salesforce, Teams and ServiceNow, where the work already happens.

“Will security sign this off?”

Human approval, an audit trail and a kill switch from the first sprint. Our approach has been cleared by a Dutch real-estate operator and a UK payments institution.

“What if a better model comes out next year?”

We're model-neutral. Claude, Copilot, Agentforce, OpenAI or UiPath, and the logic ports when something better arrives.

“Who looks after it once it's live?”

A named owner for every agent, and a monthly report on what it's delivered.

We hold ourselves to the same standard. In four months we built eight internal apps with Claude and retired five SaaS tools.

FAQ

What are examples of AI in business automation?
Common examples are matching payments to invoices, drafting quotes from CRM data, triaging service cases, capturing supplier tenders and processing claims without manual entry.
How does AI help with operations management?
AI agents take on the repetitive steps in operations, such as tracking orders, updating records and flagging exceptions, so people focus on the decisions.
Do you only work in these industries?
No. These are where we have the most depth. The approach works wherever a process runs on data and repeats.
