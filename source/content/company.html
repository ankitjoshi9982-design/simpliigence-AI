AA

Arjun Ahlawat

Chief Commercial Officer

Arjun leads go-to-market, partnerships and the advisory board at Simpliigence AI, making sure every engagement starts with the outcome a client needs and ends with one they can measure. He brings more than 20 years of building client relationships and new markets across the US, Europe and India, with leadership roles at Cognizant, Aeris and Marlabs, and most recently at Ciklum, where clients began buying AI as an ongoing service rather than a one-time project. It’s the same model Simpliigence AI is built on: agents paid on the result they deliver, not the hours behind them. Arjun has spent much of his career with life sciences and CPG companies.

VD

Vivin Deshpande

Chief Technology Officer and Co-Founder

Vivin has spent nearly two decades designing and integrating CRM systems, from Siebel at Accenture and IBM to Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics. He co-founded Simpliigence in 2019 to help growing businesses close what he calls the potential-promise gap: the distance between what their CRM could do and what it actually delivers. As CTO, he leads technology and governance across our delivery. He designed our AI-driven delivery lifecycle, where AI drafts and a person approves, and architects the agent builds now in production for clients in real estate and payments.
