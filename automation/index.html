FAQ

How does AI workflow automation work?
AI workflow automation adds a layer that reads context, decides the next step and acts across your systems, so it handles the exceptions that break rule-based automation.
RPA or an AI agent, which do I need?
It depends on how much the process changes. Stable, high-volume steps suit RPA; work that shifts or needs judgment suits an agent.
What do we pay before results show up?
A retainer that covers setup and running. The success fee is paid only when the agreed KPI improves.
What if the number doesn't move?
Then the success fee isn't paid. That's the point of the model.
What happens after the measurement window?
Most clients keep the process running with us on a renewed target, or move to a managed service.
