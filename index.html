01Prioritising
“Where do we start?”

So many processes could use AI, and every one has a champion. Without a clear way to rank them, the most valuable work waits while the loudest request moves first.

The cost: effort spreads thin. Gartner expected at least 30% of generative AI projects to be abandoned after proof of concept by the end of 2025, with unclear business value among the reasons.4

02Scaling
“The pilot worked. Now what?”

A promising demo is a long way from something hundreds of people rely on. Security, integrations and ownership all need answers before it can grow.

The cost: good ideas stall. The average company scraps 46% of its AI proofs of concept before they reach production.3

03Time to value
“When will we see a return?”

Long programmes ask for patience before anyone sees a number. Meanwhile the hours and the money keep slipping.

The cost: momentum fades. 60% of senior leaders outside the C-suite expect scaling barriers to take a year or more to clear.5

04Aligning with business outcomes
“Is this moving what matters?”

AI goals and business goals drift apart easily. Activity gets measured, results don't, and the next step gets harder to justify.

The cost: spend without a story. Half of CEOs say the pace of AI investment has left them with disconnected technology, and only 39% of organisations see any EBIT impact from AI.21

If any of these sound like your week, you're in good company. They're the reason Simpliigence AI exists.

Simpliigence AI is for teams in any industry that want AI to deliver a measurable result and need help prioritising, scaling, reaching value sooner or tying AI to business goals, using the platforms they already have.
Why do businesses need AI automation?
Work grows faster than headcount. AI automation lets a team handle more at the same quality, without adding people to repetitive tasks.
What does “outcomes, by design” mean?
It means we agree the result first, design the system around it, and are paid when that result shows up.
What is an AI automation platform?
An AI automation platform is the layer that lets AI read your data, decide the next step and act across your systems. We build that layer on the tools you already run.
