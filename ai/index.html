FAQ

What are enterprise AI solutions?
Enterprise AI solutions are software that reasons over your business data to support real decisions and actions, deployed inside your existing systems and security rules.
Do we need to buy a new AI platform?
No. We build on the model and platforms you already license.
Does our data leave our environment?
No. Everything runs inside your tenant and region, with your existing permissions.
How do payments work?
The Health Check is a half-day session. The Discovery Sprint is fixed and credited to the build. The build is paid at evaluation gates.
Can we switch models later?
Yes. The logic is designed to port, so a better model is an upgrade, not a rebuild.
