Company

An AI-first company with six years of delivery behind it.

Simpliigence AI was built to do one thing well: put AI agents to work and stand behind the result. We bring the depth of Simpliigence with us.

Our story

Simpliigence has spent six years helping more than 125 companies get more from Salesforce, data and automation. Along the way, clients started asking for something different: not another pilot, but agents that run every day and a partner willing to be measured on them. Simpliigence AI is our answer. We test everything on ourselves first. In four months we built eight internal apps with Claude and retired five SaaS tools.
